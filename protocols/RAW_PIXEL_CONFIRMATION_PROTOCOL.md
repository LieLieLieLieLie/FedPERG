# Frozen Raw-Pixel Confirmation Protocol

The raw-input confirmation trains the same four-block GroupNorm CNN on the
standard MNIST split for 100 communication rounds. AvgM+Gate, Router-only, and
FedPERG use paired seeds 60--64, 20 clients, 8 participants per round, identical
initialization and local sampling, and the same disjoint 4% server calibration
pool. The candidate bank, coefficients, 0.9 incoming momentum, and 0.001 margin
are unchanged from the fixed-feature confirmatory protocol.
